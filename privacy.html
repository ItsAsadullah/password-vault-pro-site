Password Vault Pro

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Password Vault Pro ("the extension") is a password manager for Google Chrome. This policy explains what data the extension handles and how. In short: your vault is encrypted on your device and we never receive, see or store any of your data.

1. Data the extension stores

  • Vault contents — logins, passwords, cards, identities, notes, Wi-Fi details, folders, tags and 2FA secrets you choose to save.
  • Settings — preferences such as auto-lock time, theme and sites you chose never to save.
  • Your Google account email — to show which account your vault syncs with.

2. How your vault is protected

  • Your vault is encrypted on your own device with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is protected by your master password (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 iterations) and by your personal recovery key.
  • Your master password, recovery key and decrypted data are never sent anywhere. While unlocked, the key is kept only in the browser's memory and is erased when the vault locks or the browser closes.
  • We do not operate any server. There is no account with us, and we have no way to read or recover your data.

3. Google Drive and Google sign-in

The extension uses Google sign-in only to sync your encrypted vault to your own Google Drive. It requests these permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata — to create, read, update and delete the extension's own encrypted vault file and backup snapshots in the hidden "app data" folder of your Google Drive. The extension cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email — to read your Google account email address so it can be displayed in the extension.

Data received from Google APIs is used only to provide the sync and backup feature to you. It is not transferred to anyone else, not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train any AI or machine-learning model. Password Vault Pro's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Other network requests

  • Breach check (optional). When you click "Check for data breaches", the extension asks the Have I Been Pwned service whether a password appeared in known breaches. Only the first 5 characters of the password's SHA-1 hash are sent (k-anonymity). The password itself never leaves your device.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party scripts are used.

5. Websites you visit

To offer autofill and to ask whether to save a password, the extension reads login forms on the pages you visit. This happens only on your device. Website addresses and form contents are never sent to us or to anyone else.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any personal data. We do not receive your data in the first place.

7. Your control and deletion

  • You can export or delete your vault at any time from the extension's Settings.
  • "Delete vault" permanently removes the vault and all backups from your Google Drive and from the device.
  • You can revoke the extension's Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also remove its hidden data in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps.

8. Children

The extension is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
