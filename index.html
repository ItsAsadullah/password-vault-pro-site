Password Vault Pro

Your passwords. Encrypted. In your own Google Drive.

Password Vault Pro is a zero-knowledge password manager for Google Chrome. Everything is encrypted on your device with your master password before it is synced to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive. There is no server and no account with us.

Zero-knowledge

AES-256-GCM encryption with a key derived from your master password (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 rounds). Nobody else can read your data.

Your own Google Drive

An encrypted copy is stored in the hidden app data folder of your Google Drive, with daily backups. The extension cannot see any of your other files.

Autofill & save

Fill logins on the right websites only, save new passwords, generate strong ones and keep 2FA codes in one place.

Recovery key

A personal recovery key lets you regain access if you ever forget your master password.

Google permissions

  • drive.appdata — store and read the extension's own encrypted vault file in your Drive's hidden app data folder.
  • userinfo.email — show which Google account your vault is synced with.
